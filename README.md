# Crypto Clicker

An idle clicker game for iPhone and Apple Watch, built with SwiftUI and the iOS 26 Liquid Glass design.
Tap the coin, build a mining empire, power it up to keep earning while you're away, turn your steps into
coins, trade tokens, and prestige for permanent bonuses and perks.

## Features

- **Mining.** Tap the coin (coins shower down as you do), then buy miners, from a Chromebook (a coin
  every four seconds) to a Space Mining Colony, and after your first prestige, three more beyond it. A
  Desktop costs ten times as much as a Chromebook and mines eight times as much, so after two or three
  Chromebooks it's the better buy, and the Shop marks whichever miner adds the most for its price as
  Best value. Each unit costs 15% more than the last. Owning 25, 50, 100, 200 and every 100 up to 1,000
  of a miner doubles its output. Under the coin, slim cards show your miners and power-ups, your crypto
  and your steps; tap one to open the Shop or its Tokens page. Until the first of them appears, the
  coin is bigger. Your coins per second and per tap sit just under the balance on the home screen, and
  in a bar at the bottom of the other tabs; tapping anywhere on that bar shows where the coins come
  from.
- **Mk upgrades.** At 25, 50, 100, 200 and 400 owned, a miner's next Mk upgrade (Mk II to Mk VI)
  appears in the Shop. Each doubles that miner for the rest of the run.
- **Whole coins.** Every tap, step and reward pays whole coins. Amounts are written out in full up to
  9,999 and abbreviated from 10,000 (12.3K, 1.25M, 12.5B). Tap the balance on the home screen to
  switch between the short form and every digit; the same switch is on the Income page and in
  Settings › Appearance. The coins each tap earns show the same way, and the choice is remembered.
- **Taps that matter.** Tapping upgrades make every tap also earn a share of a second's production, up to
  a whole second's worth late in the game. Quick taps build a combo: every 4.4 quick taps add ×0.1, up
  to twice the coins per tap. Automatic taps are worth a quarter of a tap, so tapping yourself stays the
  fastest way to mine.
- **Combo power-ups.** Three power-ups bought in levels in the Shop and kept through prestiges. Combo
  Booster raises the biggest combo by ×0.5 a level up to ×5 (its first level costs about 74K coins), then
  Combo Overdrive by ×1 a level up to ×10 (from about 15B). Quick Combos makes each ×0.1 come 50% sooner
  a level, up to four times as fast (from about 175K). The Combo Master perk doubles that speed again
  and lifts the biggest combo to at least ×3.
- **Upgrades.** 22 one-time upgrades. They boost tapping, multiply production (the first two by ×1.75,
  ASIC Chips and the Geothermal Plant by ×1.5, the rest by ×2), add automatic taps, and raise step and
  mini-game rewards. Upgrades are revealed as you progress (mini-game ones once you've unlocked a game).
- **Offline Mining.** At first, miners stop a minute after you leave the app. The Offline Mining
  power-up keeps them earning while it's closed: 30 minutes more per level up to a day, then an hour more
  per level, up to three days. Power-ups stay when you prestige. A welcome-back screen shows what you
  earned, what that unlocked (achievements, prestige points, finished quests), the best thing to buy
  now with a button to buy it, and a breakdown by miner, time and multiplier.
- **Airdrops.** Every 3–6 minutes an airdrop falls. Catch it within 20 seconds for a pile of coins, or for
  a 60-second Bull Run that multiplies production ×7 (the screen's edges burn red while it lasts, at
  half strength along the bottom third so the buttons stay clear). One in twenty is a whale worth up to
  an hour of income (early in a run, no more than the run has mined so far, so a lucky whale can't skip
  the opening).
  Statistics counts the ones caught and missed and lists the last ten with what each paid.
- **Daily.** From the second day: a daily reward with a streak that builds to an hour of income on day
  7, and three new quests every day. The first day is for learning to mine, without big handouts.
- **Daily challenge.** From the second day, each day picks a challenge in one of your games: a score in
  Block Chain (dealt the same pieces for everyone), a miner in Merge Mining, coins in Hash Chain, blocks in
  Hash Smash, bubbles, a Pump and Dump run, a Classic score in Token Swap, invaders shot in Scam Invaders,
  good hands at the casino (or a natural 21 at 21 Million), or points in three different arcade games.
  It pays 15 minutes of income, and five in a week pay two hours more.
- **Tokens.** The Shop's second page: ten tokens, from Shiba Inu up through Tether, Dogecoin, XRP, BNB,
  Monero, Cardano, Solana and Ethereum to Bitcoin. Token prices drift every five minutes, within about a
  quarter of their usual price, with news that nudges them up or down; Tether, a stablecoin, barely moves.
  Buy tokens (×1, ×10, ×100 or as many as you can afford) to bet or to sell later (selling costs a 10%
  fee). Tokens reset when you prestige. Each token's picture is a PNG in the app's asset catalog (see
  [Token pictures](#token-pictures)).
- **Steps.** After 2,000 taps, connect Apple Health and every step you take, on iPhone or Apple Watch,
  is paid out exactly once, as you walk, and counts toward quests and achievements. Only the game's
  first day waits: its steps are paid the next day, at the step value then, so walking can't skip the
  opening. A step is worth at least one coin and grows with your income.
- **Apple Watch.** Tap to mine from your wrist, watch your balance tick up live, and see today's steps
  and what they're worth.
- **Casino.** Bet tokens at 21 Million, Crypto Slots, Diamond Hands or To the Moon, or switch 21 Million,
  Diamond Hands and To the Moon to Practice and play the same game for chips, with nothing at stake.
  21 Million (blackjack, named for Bitcoin's 21 million coins) has two tables (the Tough dealer hits soft
  17, and wins there pay 10% more coins) and an optional coach that outlines the basic-strategy move.
  Diamond Hands is video poker, Jacks or Better on the classic 8/5 table: HODL the cards you believe in
  and draw once (Hint HODLs what a strong player would); played well it returns about 97%, and a royal
  flush pays 800 to 1. Crypto Slots lights up the reels that
  win and shows the bet, payout and net of every spin; a Cardano, a Solana and an Ethereum win three
  free spins, and every tenth Bitcoin on the line pays a collection prize (about 95.6% back over time,
  free spins included). In To the Moon the multiplier climbs up a price chart until the market
  crashes: cash out in time (or set an automatic cash-out), and every target returns 97% on average.
  Wins pay tokens plus a coin bonus, with confetti and a shower of coins. Every round is paid once.
- **Unlocking games.** The twelve games unlock one at a time. 21 Million and Block Chain cost a few thousand
  coins, and each game after costs several times the one before, up to about one and a half trillion
  for Scam Invaders: a player who keeps playing gets the first two in the first quarter hour and about
  seven in three hours, and one who checks in four times a day gets a new one every half day or so,
  the last around day four, before the first prestige.
- **Arcade.** Block Chain, Merge Mining, Hash Chain, Hash Smash, Bubble Market, Pump and Dump, Token Swap
  and Scam Invaders pay coins for every point, and 50% more for a new best score. Each is tuned so a
  minute of skilled play pays about two minutes of income. Every action game opens on a start screen
  of cards, one for each way to play and one for its settings, and the game starts from there. Each
  has a backdrop of its own on both screens, the way the casino has its felt: chains of blocks, a
  circuit board, falling hashes, a neon horizon, rising bubbles, a nebula, a pump-and-dump chart and a
  wallpaper of token signs. In the Arcade list every game shows the same two records (its best and one
  more), and a Stats button under Play opens its card from Statistics.
  - **Block Chain:** stack coin blocks (round chips in token colors, each with its token's sign) into
    full rows in a well 18 rows deep. Endless gets faster every 10 rows, and every 15 pieces a gas spike
    pushes the stack up a row from below, with one gap under your lowest column: clearing a row with gas
    in it pays a fee on top. Four rows at once is a Full Block. Also Block Rush, 40 rows against the
    clock, and Today's Pieces, Endless dealt the same for everyone. Hold, a dotted marker where the piece
    will land, and combos. The score sits at the top and the well at the bottom, with the mempool
    between: the next three pieces, the held one, and gauges for the next gas spike and level (or the
    rows to go and the best Rush).
  - **Merge Mining:** slide miners together to upgrade them up the Shop's ladder, from a Chromebook (2)
    to the Lunar Base (2,048), which reaches the Moon and pays ten minutes of income on top. Goals to
    climb, three undos a game that can't reroll a miner, and a Daily Puzzle with the same start for
    everyone, 150 moves and three stars to earn. Its settings pick how fast miners slide and whether
    each shows its number.
  - **Hash Chain:** steer a chain of blocks to mine coins, each adding a block. Classic in four arenas
    (Open, Mining Farm, Exchange Floor and Node Network), shown in miniature on the start screen, or a
    one-minute Coin Rush, with Lightning (coins count double), Cold Storage, Hardware Wallet and Airdrop
    Magnet power-ups.
  - **Hash Smash:** smash hash blocks with the coin ball: cold storage, liquidation (they set off a
    cascade) and airdrop blocks, Fork, Deep Liquidity, Gas Limit and Hash Rate power-ups, and every fifth
    level the whale, which dumps red candles that shrink your paddle. Levels from level 1, Random Walls
    made up for every game (about as hard and paying about the same), and the ball's speed.
  - **Bubble Market:** fire token bubbles (each color a token, with its sign) to pop three of a kind
    before the market sinks to the crash line. Waves add tokens and grow deeper, a streak adds 10% a pop
    (up to double) and earns Stablecoins (they match anything) and Flash Crashes (they wipe out everything
    nearby), and blue chips pay extra.
  - **Scam Invaders:** rows of meme coins, pump bots and rug pullers march down; drag to move the ship,
    which fires while your finger is down. Hits in a row add 10% each (up to double), four server racks
    shield you and crumble server by server, a whale crosses the top with bonus points and a power-up
    (Rapid Fire, Spread Shot, Laser or Shield), one golden invader a wave pays double, and every fifth
    wave is the Bear, a boss with a health bar that gives back a lost ship. Each wave starts lower and
    marches faster. Waves from wave 1, Random Waves with a formation made up for every wave (diamonds,
    arrows, frames, skylines, even one giant invader, paying about the same), and the speed.
  - Hash Chain and Merge Mining are played with swipes anywhere on the screen.
  - Block Chain, Hash Chain, Hash Smash, Scam Invaders and Pump and Dump set their speed on their start
    screens (Slow, Normal or Fast): a point is worth the same at every speed, so Fast scores sooner and
    Slow is relaxed. Every action game pauses, and a swipe in from the left edge leaves it.
- **Pump and Dump.** Tap the coins as they list on an exchange board, before they're delisted, round
  after round: each 30-second round has a score goal to reach and a bonus objective (a combo, golden
  coins, or no mistakes), and each is quicker than the last, with more scam tokens and rug pulls (coins
  with a red rim that shake, then turn). Its start screen picks the board, 3×3, 4×4 or 5×5, each shown
  in miniature with its best, and the speed. Bigger boards list coins faster and more at once, with
  bigger goals; their points pay a smaller share each (82% and 70%), so every board pays about the same
  a minute, and each keeps its own best.
- **Token Swap.** Match-three with crypto tokens, with four specials: a 2×2 square makes a Propeller
  (it flies to what you need), four in a row a Rocket (its row or column), an L or T a Bomb (a 5×5
  blast) and five in a row a Color Clear (a whole color). Swap two specials together to combine them.
  Play 18 levels with goals and move limits (ice, chains, crates and gold bars to bring down), each
  star paying coins the first time; a daily puzzle with the same board for everyone, paid once a day;
  random levels made up from a seed in three difficulties, never the same twice, with a bonus for each
  win; or Classic, a minute to score as much as you can.
- **How to Play.** In the Settings tab: a guide to every part of the game in five groups (Mining,
  Rewards, Tokens and Games, Progress, Beyond the App), each topic a line and a few short points, then
  the full rules of every mini-game. Each game also explains its goal, controls, scoring,
  specials and hazards, how it ends, and a few tips, opening by itself the first time, from a button in
  the game, and from the pause card. A tip on the home screen points new players to it.
- **Arcade Rank.** Finishing games earns XP, with more for a new best, the first game of each kind
  each day, the daily challenge, stars and game achievements (casino rounds earn a little, up to a
  daily cap). Ranks carry titles and, with achievements, unlock looks in Style, at least one kind for
  every game: card backs, the 21 Million and Diamond Hands tables, slot reels, the To the Moon ride (a
  rocket, a Lambo or a flying saucer), Block Chain coins, Merge Mining tiles (Classic, Midnight, Gold
  Rush), Hash Chain blocks, the Hash Smash paddle, Bubble Market bubbles, the Pump and Dump board, Token
  Swap tiles and the Scam Invaders ship. XP never pays coins. Playing six different games in a week pays 30 minutes of income once a week, and
  quests include winning Token Swap levels, clearing Pump and Dump rounds, reaching Merge Mining goals
  and shooting invaders.
- **Trophies.** 65 achievements with bronze, silver, gold and mythic tiers, each card glinting in the
  metal it has earned. Each star adds 1% production forever, and stars unlock new looks for the coin.
- **The coin's looks.** In Settings › Appearance, achievement stars unlock three looks for the coin:
  eight finishes (gold to holographic), eight rims (a glow, neon, a beating pulse, orbiting sparks, a
  ring of fire, a turning rainbow and glinting diamond facets) and seven tap effects (a ripple, sparks,
  a burst of little coins, rockets lifting off, lightning and a swirl of hashes), each with a big
  preview to try it. With Reduce Motion on, the rims hold still and taps set off nothing extra.
  The Goals page lists what to aim for next, each with how close it is: the next miner, the nearest
  milestone, the cheapest upgrade and game, the star closest to done, the next Arcade Rank and Token
  Swap level, and the next prestige.
- **Prestige.** Once you've mined 15 billion coins in all, start a new run for prestige points. Points
  grow with the cube root of lifetime coins, so each prestige takes longer than the last. Every point
  adds 5% production forever, and points buy permanent perks in three branches (Mining, Tapping and
  Fortune); the last few take about three weeks to afford. The Shop suggests prestiging once it would
  make you at least half as fast again: for someone who checks in four times a day, around day five.
  Tokens reset, and so does anything riding on them (a bet, a poker hand, a flight, free spins).
- **Difficulty.** In Settings, each with what it does in a line (an info button explains the rest): Easy
  mines 50% more but prestiges earn half the points; Hard mines 40% less and prestiges earn 50% more.
  Everything that follows income moves with it; prices don't. A run's prestige points use the easiest
  difficulty picked since it began.
- **Notifications.** Optional reminders when Offline Mining runs out and when a daily reward is waiting.
- **Statistics.** In Settings: a chart of income over the last day, week or month, or all time (a point
  a minute, with times in hours and minutes at first, then hours, days and months as the range grows,
  and a scale in steps of ten once income has grown a hundredfold), plus everything the game counts
  (win streaks, biggest wins, what the casino paid back, records for every game), in short form or with
  every digit. Each game has a card with its best score (or biggest win) up top and its records as
  tiles; miners show their Mk upgrade and share of income, tokens what they're worth now, and airdrops
  what they've paid. Your Journey lists when each first happened: every miner tier, each game, Offline
  Mining, every thousandfold of coins and the first prestige. Tapping the rates under the balance (or
  the bar at the bottom of the other tabs) opens the Income page: each miner's share, automatic taps,
  multipliers and what makes up a tap, with the switch between short numbers and every digit.
- **Siri and Shortcuts.** "How many coins do I have in Crypto Clicker?" answers with your balance and
  income, brought up to now, without opening the game.
- **Languages.** Every piece of text players see sits in String Catalogs, ready to translate: one for the
  app, the watch and the widgets each (Xcode adds the text from the screens as it builds), and one in
  CryptoClickerKit for the game's own text (names, rules, quests, achievements), which the kit reads
  through `localized(…)`. English is the only language so far.
- **Widgets and the Live Activity.** The Balance widget shows the coin (in your finish) and your balance on
  the Home Screen (small and medium) or the Lock Screen (rectangular, circular or inline), the number
  growing as your miners earn, until they stop. A Live Activity shows the same on the Lock Screen and in
  the Dynamic Island while the game is closed: the balance, coins a second, a countdown and a bar for how
  long the miners keep going, and the balance they'll reach; once they stop it shows what's waiting to be
  collected. It can be turned off in Settings › Lock Screen and Widgets.

## Requirements

- Xcode 26 or later
- iOS 26 / iPadOS 26 and watchOS 26
- Automatic signing. The app and its widgets share the app group `group.Hilal-Safi.Crypto-Clicker`, which
  Xcode registers when it signs them; if it reports the group isn't set up, tick it under App Groups in
  each of the two targets' Signing & Capabilities.

## Architecture

- **One value holds the game.** `GameState` is a plain `Codable` struct. Every rule is a method on it in
  CryptoClickerKit, so the rules can be tested without a device and both apps share them.
- **GameStore** is the app's single `@Observable` model. It credits income on a 5 Hz clock, saves the
  game as an atomic JSON file in Application Support, schedules airdrops, refreshes the market and the
  day's quests, schedules reminders, and connects Apple Health and the watch. Views read `store.game`
  and call store actions. The game itself only republishes about once a second as the clock ticks (and
  at once for anything the player does), while the live balance, `store.coins`, changes every tick, so
  only the screens showing the balance redraw five times a second. It's split by area into
  `GameStore+Actions`, `+Games`, `+Progress`, `+Services` and `+Debug`; the rules it used to hold (the
  save file and its recovery, the airdrop schedule, how a returning player is greeted) live in the kit,
  where they're tested.
- **Time.** The live clock and returning from the background use the same `advance(to:)` path. No time is
  paid twice or lost. The clock only runs while the app is on screen, so the Offline Mining limit applies
  to the whole absence even when the watch wakes the phone in the background.
- **The market needs no saving.** Prices, charts and news are worked out from the clock with a stable
  hash, so every device sees the same market and the past can be charted. Daily quests, the daily
  challenge and Block Day's Tetris pieces are seeded the same way.
- **One place per game.** Each mini-game's name, icon, color, price, pitch and pay rate live in
  `MiniGame.info`; its records come from the statistics in one place, so adding a game starts there.
  Every action game's screen shares one lifecycle helper for pausing, holding a run in the background
  and paying out on the way out, and one backdrop modifier for its look.
- **Saves move forward.** Prestige values keep the names they were saved under when prestiging was called
  a Hard Fork, and each new save version carries older prestige progress over to the current scale, so
  earlier saves load unchanged.
- **Safety nets.** An unfinished Blackjack bet or To the Moon flight is saved and refunded if the app
  closes mid-round (leaving a flight normally cashes it out first), and an unfinished Video Poker hand
  is played out with the suggested holds. An arcade game left mid-run is paid on the next launch, with
  its records and Arcade XP. Watch taps are written to disk the moment they arrive.
  A save that can't be read yet (before the first unlock after a restart) is never overwritten. One
  that can't be read at all is set aside under a new name, and the app asks whether to try again or
  start a new game; nothing is saved until you choose.
- **Steps.** The iPhone reads daily totals from HealthKit statistics queries, which merge iPhone and Apple
  Watch samples without double counting. A ledger remembers how many steps of each day have been paid,
  so syncing is safe to repeat at any time and late-arriving data is still paid.
- **Apple Watch.** The phone sends the watch a snapshot: balance, income rates, any active boost and
  step info. The watch projects the balance forward on its own. Watch taps travel back as batches through
  `transferUserInfo`, which is queued, ordered and survives either app quitting. Each watch numbers its
  batches, and the phone applies each number once and sends back the last number it applied, so the
  watch clears exactly what's been paid, however many batches arrive together. (Batches from older
  versions are still matched by ID.)
- **Widgets and the Live Activity.** The same idea as the watch: the app leaves a `BalanceSnapshot`
  (balance, income, any boost, the Offline Mining limit and the coin's finish, anchored at the game clock)
  in the app group `group.Hilal-Safi.Crypto-Clicker` when it opens, about once a minute while it's open, and
  as it goes to the background, and reloads the widgets. A widget's timeline then projects the balance
  every five minutes or so until the miners stop, by the same rule the game credits time away with, so
  it never needs to wake the app. The Live Activity gets the snapshot as its state, with a stale date at
  the moment the miners stop; its countdown and bar run on the system's timers. It starts while the app
  is open (iOS only allows that) and updates as the app goes to the background.

## Tests

Run the game-logic suite from the package folder:

```sh
cd CryptoClickerKit
swift test
```

In Xcode, Product › Test (⌘U) on the Crypto Clicker scheme runs the same suite. It includes simulated
players that check the economy's pacing. They play the way people do: they tap, catch most airdrops,
claim quests and daily rewards, play some arcade, earn achievements, unlock games, prestige when the
Shop suggests it and buy perks, combo power-ups included. A player who keeps playing buys a Chromebook
in the first minute, a Desktop after three Chromebooks at about two minutes, a GPU Rig after about a
quarter of an hour, a Data Center after a little over an hour and six miner tiers in three hours,
without a prestige point yet. A light player, who looks in for four minutes five times in twelve hours,
has mined about a hundred thousand coins by then, mostly while away. A casual player who checks in four
times a day buys Offline Mining in the first session, reaches the Data Center in about a day and the
first prestige point around day two, has every game in about four days, and gets the tenth miner and
the first suggestion to prestige around day five. With prestiges and perks, new things keep arriving
for about three weeks.

Simulated skilled players for every arcade game check that each pays about the same for a minute of
play (about two minutes of income), Token Swap included. They take a few minutes, so they run only when asked:

```sh
RUN_BALANCE_TESTS=1 swift test --filter ArcadeBalanceTests
```

The same switch adds a three-week casual run to the pacing tests, checking that perks keep coming
through the third week:

```sh
RUN_BALANCE_TESTS=1 swift test --filter PacingTests
```

A GitHub Actions workflow (`.github/workflows/kit-tests.yml`) builds the package and runs the suite on
pushes to `main` and `refactor/` branches and on pull requests; run it by hand from the Actions tab to
include the balance tests.

## Testing tools

Builds run from Xcode (the Debug configuration) include a Testing Tools screen, tucked away at the
bottom of the Settings tab. It adds coins (up to 1 sextillion at a time), tokens, miners, upgrades,
Offline Mining levels, every combo power-up and prestige points, unlocks every mini-game and steps, completes the day's quests or starts a new day (which also brings the dailies forward on the
first day), starts a Bull Run, drops an airdrop or a whale, and pretends you were away for an hour or
eight. The screen and its code are compiled out of Release builds, so they never reach the App Store.

## Token pictures

Each token's picture is a PNG in `Crypto Clicker/Assets.xcassets`, in an image set named after the
token. To change one, replace the PNG inside its folder and keep the file name (or drag the new picture
onto the image set in Xcode):

| Token | Image set | File |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tether | `tether_image.imageset` | `Tether.png` |
| BNB | `bnb_image.imageset` | `BNB.png` |
| Monero | `monero_image.imageset` | `Monero.png` |

Tether, BNB and Monero ship with simple placeholder coins until their real pictures go in. A square
PNG of at least 256 × 256 with a transparent background looks best; the other tokens' sets show the
same layout.
